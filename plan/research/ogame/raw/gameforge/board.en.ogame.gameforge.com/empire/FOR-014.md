# FAQ — Colonization

What the publisher states about taking a second planet and where it may be placed. This is the
gating a placement decision has to respect — how much astrophysics buys how many colonies, and
which slots those levels unlock — so a colony plan can be refused locally instead of being sent and
rejected by the host.

## Rule-bearing lines (verbatim)

- To build a colony ship, you will need impulse drive level 3, and 10,000 metal, 20,000 crystal, and 10,000 deuterium.
- To colonize a second planet, astrophysics must be at least level 1. For each further planet, you will have to research 2 extra levels of astrophysics.
- It will only be possible to colonize a planet if there is no other player in that position, hence the line is empty, and if the level of astrophysics is sufficient for the position (with level 1: positions 4 to 12, with level 4: positions 3 – 13, with level 6: positions 2 to 14, with level 8: any position).
- The Discoverer class increases the planet size by 10% (it's 10% of the size = diameter, and not 10% of the number of fields).
- The Researchers class (alliance) increases the planet size by 5% (it's 5% of the size = diameter, and not 5% of the number of fields).
- The number of fields may differ according to universe settings.
