# What this source is good for

The highest-authority *accessible* statement in this pass for two mechanics: the **cost table** of every
defence object, and the **70 % defence repair** rule. It is a Gameforge content-marketing page (EN-GB),
not the rulebook and not a tutorial thread, so it carries `DOCUMENTED` weight only where it states a
mechanic, and no weight at all as doctrine.

## Mechanics claims (verbatim)

> Defensive structures are stationary units that protect a planet without requiring fleetsave management.

> The biggest advantage of defence over a protective fleet is regeneration: 70% of all destroyed
> defensive structures are automatically restored after combat. Anyone who builds strong defence only
> needs to replace 30% of losses in an emergency.

> Anti-Ballistic Missiles neutralise those attacks in a 1:1 ratio.

> Defence is no substitute for fleetsave — but an indispensable deterrent for any player who regularly
> leaves their planet unattended.

## Composition / build-order advice (verbatim — school A, and explicitly loose)

> **Early Phase: Deterrent Against Beginners** — In the early phase, Rocket Launchers and Light Lasers
> suffice as initial deterrents […] However, having sufficient resources for research and fleet building
> is more important than extensive defence in this phase.

> **Mid-Game: Mass Deployment and Shield Domes** — The Small Shield Dome is the first important building
> block: it protects all other structures with a shared shield pool and significantly increases the
> survivability of the entire defence. This is followed by Gauss Cannons as anti-battleship structures
> and a Large Shield Dome.

> **Endgame: Plasma Turrets and Complete Coverage** — A planet with 50+ Plasma Turrets, Shield Domes and
> Gauss Cannons is simply not profitable for most fleeters to attack.

> For miner players (Collector class), strong defence is essential. For active fleeters, fleetsave is
> more important, but they too build basic defence against opportunistic attacks during shorter offline
> phases.

**Note for synthesis:** the only number in this page's build order is "50+ Plasma Turrets" in the
endgame, with no stockpile size, no techs and no attacker defined. It is a marketing number
(`ANECDOTAL`), and the page names no ratio at all — do not cite it as a composition rule.
