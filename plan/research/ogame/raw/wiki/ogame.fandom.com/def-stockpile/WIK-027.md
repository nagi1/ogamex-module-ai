# What this source is good for

The **stockpile-protection taxonomy**: the three general ways to keep resources out of an attacker's
reach.

## Definition (verbatim)

> **Resource Saving** (also know as "RS") refers to techniques used to save resources from attackers
> or being seen by espionage probes.

## The three methods (verbatim, trimmed)

1. "Sending the resources along on a fleetsaving mission. This will result in the resources being
   relocated, when fleetsaving to a colony, unless the fleet is recalled."
2. "Sending the resources along on a debris field harvest mission. Has the advantage of not
   relocating the resources."
3. "Beginning a research project or building. Since research and building immediately earmarks those
   resources, they will be saved until the research/building is canceled. However all resources will
   be 'lost' (i.e. used) if not canceled before it completes."

## Claims

- **DOCUMENTED** — the third method "would be useful, for example, if someone attacked and the fleet
  was still returning from a fleetsave, lost or otherwise unavailable, because this tactic requires
  no ships. Just start something before the attack and then cancel it moments after."
- **DOCUMENTED** — scale caveat: the third method "generally means that at low levels, fewer
  resources can be saved and for shorter times, but it ultimately depends on the research/building."
- **DOCUMENTED** — "Deconstructing buildings can also save resources, if there are not enough to
  upgrade."
- **DOCUMENTED** — the page stresses resources must be hidden from **espionage probes** as well as
  attackers (the "being seen" half of the definition).

## Numbers

- **No numbers** are stated beyond "three general methods".

## Limitations

- **CONTESTED as mechanics:** that research/building "immediately earmarks" resources, that they show
  as spent to espionage, that cancellation refunds them, and that DF-harvest keeps resources in place,
  are all community statements. Confirming needs the official Gameforge rules or the host
  implementation (build/research queue and espionage-report behaviour).
- Overlaps `Canceling` (WIK-023) and `Save` (WIK-022), and is the general case of `Resource Hiding`
  (WIK-028). The three agree; agreement is supporting, not a mechanics confirmation.
