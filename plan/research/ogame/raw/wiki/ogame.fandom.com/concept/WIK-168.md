## Rule-bearing lines (verbatim)
- The '''Research Screen''' contains a list of available [[Technology|technologies]] with pictures and descriptions.  It will say after the technologies' name what level the tech has currently been researched to.  The cost of researching the next level, as well as the amount of time it will take are shown under the description.  You can research the next level technology by clicking on the '''Research''' text to the right of the text.
