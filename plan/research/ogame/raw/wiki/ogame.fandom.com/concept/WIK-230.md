## Rule-bearing lines (verbatim)
- The '''rewards system''' runs as a 7 or 14-day event and provides tasks for players to complete. Each finished task awards Tritium. The amount of Tritium a player gains during an event serves to unlock varies levels of rewards. Each player receives one reward per level, of which there are five. Those using the [[officer|command staff]] receive additional rewards and gain 20% extra tritium for tasks. The reward system was added in December 2020 and will be run as an event periodically.
