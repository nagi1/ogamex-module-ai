## Rule-bearing lines (verbatim)
- The Defense Screen contains a list of available [[Defense]] units with pictures and descriptions.  It will say after the unit's name the number of units currently on the planet.  The cost of the defensive unit, as well as the amount of time it will take to build are shown under the description.  You can build additional units by entering the number of  units you want to build of each type and the pressing '''send''' at the bottom of the page.
- It will show a resource in Red if you do not have enough of that particular resource.  It will also tell you how long you will have to wait at current mine production before you can build that unit.
