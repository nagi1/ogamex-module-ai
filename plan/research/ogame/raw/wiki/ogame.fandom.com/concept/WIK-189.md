## Stated technique (verbatim)
- A type of alliance with a lot of members, more than 40 usually. The term is generally derogatory due to the fact that alliances with a large amount of members sometimes have difficulty in keeping track of what their members are doing, thus creating a communication issue within the alliance. Some larger alliances create wings to move low point members to, therefore making it easier to control every member. However, it has been shown that some larger alliances can become very successful.
