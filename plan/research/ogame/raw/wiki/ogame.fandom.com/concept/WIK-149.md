- An important thing to note is that '''the resource cost to research each level of a given technology is twice that of the previous level excluding [[Astrophysics]] (which is 1.75 and result is rounded to the nearest multiple of 100) and [[Graviton Technology]] (for which every level is three times as expensive)'''.
