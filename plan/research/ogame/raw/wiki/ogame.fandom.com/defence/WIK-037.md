## Rule-bearing lines (verbatim)
- Gauss Cannons are an effective early-game defence due to their high shield and weapon power, making them capable of holding their own against [[Cruiser]]-based fleets, when backed by large amounts of [[fodder]]. Gauss Cannons become less attractive as the backbone of a planet's defence as players ascend the ranks since [[Deathstar|Deathstars]] can [[Rapid Fire|rapid fire]] against them. In contrast, Deathstars can only target a single [[Plasma Turret]] during each round of battle.
- Despite being less effective against capital ships, Gauss Cannons are more cost-effective than Plasma Turrets. They are capable of destroying [[Heavy Fighter|Heavy Fighters]] in a single shot; a host of cannons are capable of wiping away a large amount of fodder for substantially fewer resources.
