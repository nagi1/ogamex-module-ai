# What this source is good for

The definition of the **fleeter** play style, and the stated obligation that a fleeter must
diversify because fleetcrashing alone no longer sustains an account.

## Position stated (verbatim, trimmed)

> A **fleeter** is a type of player that focuses on combat with other players. The main goal of every
> fleeter is to fleetcrash rival players. Income is earned through gathering the debris field of
> crashed fleets. Many view this as the ultimate play style and test of skill.

> Many fleeters first start as miners, in order to build a strong base economy and account before
> they begin fleeting.

## Claims

- **DOCUMENTED** — active style: "requiring substantial time commitment to watch and learn the habits
  and fleetsave patterns of other players."
- **DOCUMENTED** — diversification rule: "A fleeter generally is forced to diversify activities to
  also raid, run expeditions, farm inactives, and use their own mines. This is necessary to have
  enough resource income to build fleets and sustain the fleeter approach. Fleetcrashes are not often
  enough in the modern game to sustain a fleeter on their own, unless one has exceptional skill."
- **DOCUMENTED** — spend pattern: technologies to a high level (combat, ship speed, espionage, fleet
  slots); ships, chosen "to counter the specific styles of defense or fleets their rivals have
  built"; moon phalanx and jump gates; shipyard and Nanite Factory levels for build speed.
- **DOCUMENTED** — deuterium dependency: "A fleeter is generally starved for deuterium to fuel their
  ships and fleetsaves. This must usually be acquired through trade or use of the merchant." Social
  rule attached: "To build good trade partnerships, fleeters often offer a NAP to their deuterium
  suppliers."
- **DOCUMENTED** — survival skill: "A fleeter must truly master the ability to fleetsave and in
  random patterns or he or she will almost certainly get fleet crashed."

## Limitations

- **CONTESTED as mechanics:** the named technologies, moons/phalanx and shipyard roles are community
  phrasing; confirm against official Gameforge descriptions or the host code.
- **No numbers** on this page — no ratios, thresholds or costs.
- The "modern game" wording is undated; the page carries no version or Lifeform-era marker.
