# What this source is good for

The definition of the **turtle** play style, its stated purpose (a safe-house for fleets and
resources), and the community **IPM-etiquette** position (when it is considered honourable to IPM a
turtler).

## Position stated (verbatim, trimmed)

> A **turtle** or **turtling** refers in OGame to a passive defensive playing-style, with focus on
> strong defenses, rather than large fleets.

> That's why this large build of defense allows for a more 'relaxed' playing style […] It allows the
> player to be "offline" for a few days and up to a week if necessary.

## Claims

- **DOCUMENTED** — purpose: "to create a safe-house for fleets and resources or to prevent great
  loss when all the fleets have been sent, or by players who do not have that much time to play".
- **DOCUMENTED** — offline window: a few days and up to a week. Rule that follows: "Any longer will
  not endanger the turtle planet itself but the turtler's colonies which produce the resources to
  build the defenses."
- **DOCUMENTED** — placement rule: "most turtlers put their planets close to each other, preferably
  in the same solar system to shorten transport times."
- **DOCUMENTED** — the turtle population varies with universe age: "The largest turtles in the
  oldest universes have up to a million units of defense on one planet, whereas the turtles of the
  newer universes have merely 2000 units on one planet." (**ANECDOTAL** — order-of-magnitude
  observations, not a measurement; "a million units" / "2000 units" are as stated.)
- **DOCUMENTED** — stated build: "the larger turtles have discovered that building Death Stars
  behind a huge wall of defensive fodder creates an almost impenetrable wall".
- **DOCUMENTED** — stated counter: "a large volley of IPM from a group of individuals working
  together can crush even the strongest turtles".
- **ANECDOTAL / school position** — the IPM-etiquette rule: bringing down an uber-turtler takes "so
  staggering" a time/resource cost that "the turtler must have: 1 - having billion of resources and
  fleet behind his defenses, or 2 - Being very annoying or an irritating player. If one of these
  criteria does not meet it is generally considered not honorable to IPM a turtler."

## Limitations

- **CONTESTED as mechanics:** that Death Stars sit behind fodder, that IPM volleys destroy defence
  without rebuild, and the exact unit behaviour are community phrasing; confirming them needs the
  official Gameforge unit/missile descriptions or the host code.
- The 1,000,000 / 2000 unit figures are unattributed anecdotes with no universe or date attached.
- No Lifeform-era marker; the page reads as classic-era content.
