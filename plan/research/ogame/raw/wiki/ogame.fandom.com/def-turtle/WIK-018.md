# What this source is good for

The definition that distinguishes an **uber-turtle** from a turtle, and the only order-of-magnitude
picture in this batch of what a maximal defensive stockpile looks like.

## Position stated (verbatim, trimmed)

> An **Uber-Turtle** is a defensive player (turtle) that goes far beyond what he or she needs to
> defend themselves. Turtles usually have enough defense to prevent getting attacked by a foreign
> fleet over a night or two. Uber-Turtles, however, invest almost all of their income into defense.
> Their goal is to have enough defense so that it is impossible for an attacker to make a profit off
> an attack.

## Numbers as stated (verbatim)

> Some uber-turtle's defenses have been known to have up to or more than the following:

- Rocket Launchers — 5,000,000+
- Light Lasers — 4,000,000+
- Heavy Lasers — 1,000,000+
- Gauss Cannons — 150,000+
- Ion Cannons — 200,000+
- Plasma Turrets — 100,000+
- ABMs — 100+
- Death Stars — 500+ (as mobile defense)

**INFERENCE (arithmetic on the stated list, not a source claim):** the stated mix sits at roughly
RL : LL : HL : Gauss : Ion : Plasma = 50 : 40 : 10 : 1.5 : 2 : 1, i.e. RL ≈ 50× Plasma and
LL ≈ 40× Plasma. Use only as a shape indicator; the page gives no derivation.

## Limitations

- **ANECDOTAL, unattributed, undated:** "have been known to" with no universe, date or player cited;
  the `+` markers mean lower bounds only.
- **Outdated-ruleset content — recorded as a limitation:** Death Stars counted "as mobile defense"
  and an ABM total of "100+" belong to older defence/IPM practice; do not carry these as current
  rules without confirming the host ruleset.
- **CONTESTED as mechanics:** unit names and their defensive role are community phrasing; a
  mechanics claim would need the official Gameforge unit descriptions or host code.
- No ratios, costs or thresholds beyond the counts above.
