## Stated technique (verbatim)
- A '''Prat''' is a player who spends a large amount of time in vacation mode, exiting in order to make attacks on other players.
- Because this style abuses vacation mode, they are always able to avoid being hit when they do not want to be. However, the play style limits resource income and construction. Therefore, it is really only used by those with old and highly developed accounts. By sacrificing income and building ability the prat gains safety during his or her time in vacation mode and limits the time investment needed for the game.
