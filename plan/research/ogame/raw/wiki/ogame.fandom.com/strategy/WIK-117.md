## Stated technique (verbatim)
- A mobile attack base is when you set up a colony in a solar system with a lot of farms and raid them. Do not develop the colony. Send the resources back to your other planets. When you're done, send back your fleets and then destroy the undeveloped colony so you can set up another Mobile Attack Base.
