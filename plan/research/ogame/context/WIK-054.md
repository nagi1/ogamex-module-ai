## 2. Numbers stated by the source (verbatim, with line)
- `8` - The '''Ion Cannon''' is a defensive structure, well known for its relatively high [[Shield Power]]. Despite its in-game description, it does not "disable" attacking enemy ships. Since version 7, the Ion Cannon costs 5.000 Metal and 3.000 Crystal.
- `10` - The Ion Cannon has [[Rapid Fire]] against the Reaper (2).
- `11` - Also note that the Ion Cannon can absorb up to 500 damage per round which is the second highest shield power next to the Deathstar (which has 50.000) along with Destroyers and Bombers. This makes it very useful against a Cruiser or [[Heavy Fighter]] fleet since most of the attacks will be deflected by the shields. 25 Ion Cannons against a fleet of 150 Light Fighters will result in a near 100% draw with the attacker losing 65 fighters if the techs are equal. However, any ship at least as strong as a [[Battleship]] will rip through the shields and erase the shield fodder. Despite this, Ion cannons are very useful defenses even in small numbers mid game as they will draw and absorb a large amount of damage per round.
