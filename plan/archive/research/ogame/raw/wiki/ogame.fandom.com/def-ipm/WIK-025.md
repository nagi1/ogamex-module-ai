## Behaviour as stated (verbatim, trimmed)

- Launched from the Galaxy Screen; designed to destroy enemy defence; "Defense destroyed by
  Interplanetary missiles will not be rebuilt automatically."
- "In an IPM attack, only the missile's Weapon Technology and the defense's hull plating are used
  for calculation, as IPMs penetrate shields, including Small and Large Shield Domes."
- "Before an IPM can hit the defense itself, all Anti-ballistic Missiles must be destroyed. Each ABM
  destroys one IPM."
- "IPMs can be used to destroy other IPMs on a target planet. However, IPMs on the defending planet
  are not directly targetable, so all other defenses must be destroyed first. Even with
  armor/weapons, research 1 IPM destroys 8 IPMs."
- Tracking: on being counter or hitting, "a message will appear in your inbox, stating where the
  missile came from (coordinates and the player name) and how many were blocked, or how many hit the
  planet."
- "You can deconstruct both Interplanetary Missiles and Anti-ballistic Missiles."

## Formulas and numbers

- **Range (verbatim):** `Range = (5 × Impulse Drive level) − 1` systems. Example given: Impulse Drive
  **5** → `(5*5 - 1) = 24` system range ("from 1:50 you could hit up to 1:26 and 1:74").
- **Travel time (verbatim):** `(30 + 60*(systems)) / universe_speed` seconds. Examples: same-system
  flight **30 s**; `1:100:5 → 1:110:5` = **630 s** ("10 minutes and 30 seconds"). "They do not use a
  fleet slot during travel time."
- **Storage (verbatim):** "You can store **5 Interplanetary Missiles for every Missile Silo Level**."
- **IPM-vs-IPM (verbatim):** "Even with armor/weapons, research 1 IPM destroys 8 IPMs."
- **Requirements (verbatim):** Shipyard Level 1; Missile Silo Level 4; Impulse Drive level 1.
- **INFERENCE (storage ratio):** 5 IPM per silo level (below) vs 10 ABM per silo level (WIK-026) =
  a stated **1:2 IPM:ABM** capacity ratio.

## Limitations (ruleset markers — do not treat as current rules)

- **Pre-0.78c:** "Before Ogame version 0.78c the range was determined by the formula: Range = (5 x
  Impulse Drive level) - 1" (the page prints the same formula, so the "change" it flags is not
  visible here).
- **Pre-0.78a:** "Interplanetary Missiles could not be used to destroy the defense on a moon prior to
  ogame version 0.78a."
- **Redesign-era:** "In the redesign, missiles are no longer tracked on the Sensor Phalanx."
- **0.78a:** "IPMs can be used to destroy defenses on a moon and ABMs from the planet will defend the
  moon in those cases."
