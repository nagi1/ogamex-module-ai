# What this source is good for

A per-unit **cost-effectiveness evaluation** of the defence classes, plus one explicit
ABM:Plasma-Turret stocking tip. It is opinion, not a mechanic: every combat outcome below needs an
official source or a simulator to confirm.

## Page-level limitation (verbatim)

> ''Note: This evaluation does not apply for [[Clone]]s.''

**Lifeform-era limitation:** the page itself excludes a Lifeform-era defence (**Clone**), so parts of
its advice belong to a mixed ruleset. Do not apply its verdicts to Clone-style units.
Sections observed but not transcribed: `External links`.

## Per-unit positions (verbatim, trimmed)

**Rocket Launcher** — "a good, cheap, early game defense. In later stages they're only worthwhile as
fodder." Pros: "does a better job as fodder than Light Fighters at half the cost." Cons: "Unbalanced
cost, supremely weak, cruisers rapid fire (RF) cuts these down like grass."
Tip: **"40 Rocket Launchers will destroy a single destroyer in a single round."**

**Light Laser** — "a better defense than Rocket Launcher […] more balanced in cost." Cons: endgame
"very susceptible to Destroyer attacks" (Destroyers have RF against Light Lasers).

**Heavy Laser** — "for pure firepower, you're better off buying 4 Light ones." Used by advanced
players "as a pure armor fodder for IPM attacks" because "With IPMs only counting armor vs weapon
power, you can absorb many more IPMs with fewer losses with sufficient armor tech and heavy laser
counts." Pros: "One Heavy Laser can take out a Light Fighter in one shot" and "can damage Death
Stars if the weapon level is 10 or more above the attacker." Cons: "too expensive" for the firepower.

**Ion Cannon** — "exists mainly as fodder due to its high shield strength but is lacking in actual
firepower." Pros: high shield makes good fodder, "great against all ships below the Battleship
especially Cruisers and Heavy Fighters"; "They have rapid fire against Reapers." Cons: "Not Very
cost-effective."

**Gauss Cannon** — "second-to-best defense out there." Pros: "Can destroy a Cruiser in one hit and
its attack is equal to that of a Battleship"; "very balanced price for the firepower they offer, thus
making it highly profitable." Cons: "can be ravaged by swarms of fighters."

**Plasma Turret** — "the most powerful defense mechanism in OGame." Pros: "Destroys Battleships in
one hit, and 7 Plasma Turrets can draw with one Death Star (as they do not have rapid fire and only
six rounds of combat)." Cons: "Expensive and unbalanced cost. Deuterium cost inhibits building large
numbers."

## Numbers / ratios extracted

- **CONTESTED (sim/anecdote): 40 Rocket Launchers** destroy 1 Destroyer in one round.
- **INFERENCE (from the printed comparison): 1 Heavy Laser ≈ 4 Light Lasers** in pure firepower.
- **CONTESTED (threshold): Heavy Laser** can damage a Death Star only when weapon tech is
  **level 10 or more above** the attacker.
- **CONTESTED (sim): 7 Plasma Turrets** draw with **1 Death Star**; stated reason: no rapid fire and
  only six rounds of combat.
- **DOCUMENTED (tip, verbatim):** "Keep about **1-2 Anti-ballistic Missiles per turret** (topping out
  at **50-70 missiles**) as Plasma Turrets are the main reason players attack with Interplanetary
  Missiles."

## Limitations

- **Lifeform-era content:** the Clone exclusion note, and "rapid fire against Reapers" (a
