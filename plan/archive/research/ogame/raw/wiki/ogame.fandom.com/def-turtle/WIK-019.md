# What this source is good for

The definition of a **vault/main planet** (the hub a turtle or miner concentrates on), the stated
risk of concentrating there, and the explicit **mine-efficiency rule** that favours identical planets.

## Position stated (verbatim, trimmed)

> A **Vault Planet** is a planet that a player uses as his or her **main** planet, or hub. Resources
> are gathered from other planets to this location, which may have higher developed Facilities and
> Defenses.

> While a common practice, it is rather risky as any Fleeter can identify it and focus attention
> there.

## Claims

- **DOCUMENTED** — "Vault Planets are mainly used by Turtlers and Miners." For a fleeter the advice
  is the opposite: "your planets should be spread out so that you won't be frequently attacked […]
  You should have a balanced amount of buildings and ships on each planet."
- **DOCUMENTED** — a vault should generally be the biggest planet (most fields); "Home planets
  should not be a Vault, because they have limited numbers of fields"; it should carry high-level
  facilities, build most fleets/defenses and do most research, and supply resources to the other
  planets.
- **DOCUMENTED** — risk: spy reports reveal the vault, so an opponent can identify the fleet base;
  "Even if you fleetsave, knowing the planet you are using is a key towards a crash."
- **DOCUMENTED** — stated inefficiency: "if a player builds mine levels higher on this planet than
  others, it is very inefficient in use of resources."
- **DOCUMENTED** — alternate rule: "build each planet identically. This is the most efficient way to
  build mines in terms or return on investment. Keeping them the same level on all planets is the
  optimal mining approach." Plus: keep facilities equal too, and rotate the "main" planet so fleeters
  cannot guess where you operate from.

## Numbers

- **Tip (verbatim):** "It is suggested that you colonize your Vault Planet on slot position 8, as it
  has the most balanced temperature, so you can have a good amount of both Deuterium and Energy from
  Solar Satellites. Also, position 8 planets have the largest Field Count."
- **CONTESTED as mechanics:** "position 8 planets have the largest Field Count" and "most balanced
  temperature" are slot mechanics. A wiki cannot supply them; confirm against the official Gameforge
  universe-slot tables or the host code before encoding slot 8 as a rule.

## Limitations

- The **mine-efficiency rule** (identical levels per planet = optimal ROI) is the page's school
  position; it is stated, not derived — tag **DOCUMENTED** as a position, **INFERENCE** if generalised
  into an account policy.
- No costs, ratios or level thresholds are given. Only the number on this page is the slot position
  **8**.
