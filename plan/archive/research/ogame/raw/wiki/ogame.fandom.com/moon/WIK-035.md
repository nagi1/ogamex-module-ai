- Lunar bases are structures similar to [[Terraformer]]s constructed on a [[moon]] that generate three lunar fields per level, allowing for the building of structures, such as [[Jump Gate]]s and [[Sensor Phalanx]]es. Unlike terraformers, they must be constructed in order to build more structures. It is important to note that upgrading your lunar base requires an open field.
