## Rule-bearing lines (verbatim)
- For newer universes:- 0 to 50.000 points: general protection with a 1:5 ratio<br>
- - 50.000 to 500.000 points: general protection with a 1:10 ratio
- You can't attack players with more than 500% of your points or less than 20% of your points.<br>
- Players with less than 20% of your points will be displayed on the [[Galaxy View]] screen in green and will have a small "n" displayed next to their names.<br>
- Players with more than 500% of your points will be displayed on the Galaxy View screen in red and will have a small "s" displayed next to their names if you are still in newbie protection.
- You can attack anyone who has more than 20% of your score and more than 5000 points.
