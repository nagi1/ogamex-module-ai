## Rule-bearing lines (verbatim)
- If a player does not log in to OGame for one week, all the planets of that player are marked with a <tt>(i)</tt> symbol in the [[Galaxy View]]. The name of the player also becomes grey when they become inactive. In effect, the first week after the player is marked inactive their planets are marked with <tt>(i)</tt>. After an additional 3 weeks of inactivity, the player's planets are marked with an <tt>(I)</tt>, for a total of 4 weeks, or 28 days, of inactivity.
