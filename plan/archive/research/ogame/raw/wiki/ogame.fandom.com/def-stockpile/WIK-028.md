# What this source is good for

The **resource-hiding** technique (start a cheaper build/research so resources read as "spent" in
espionage, cancel before completion, repeat) and the page's stated per-object storage examples and
research ratios.

## Definition (verbatim, trimmed)

> **Resource Hiding** is a strategy that makes a planet appear as though it does not have as many
> resources as it actually does. A player might wish to do this if they are saving up for an expensive
> technology or building so that they will not be the target of raids.

## Technique as stated (verbatim, trimmed)

> […] the player starts to research a technology or build a building that requires less resources
> than their Goal […]. This way the resources are "spent" and do not show up in espionage reports.
> The player then cancels the research or building before it is fully completed. That way, the
> resources appear back on the stockpile […]. The player then does this again until the resources for
> the Goal are saved up and the Goal can be built.

- **DOCUMENTED (hard rule)** — "Don't forget to use this strategy only by researching a technology or
  building a building. If you spend your resources on ships or defense structures, you won't be able
  to get them back."
- **DOCUMENTED (threshold)** — "In order to use this technique correctly, research and building time
  should be at least **4 hours** (Days would be preferred) so that the player can stop the production
  in the amount time allowed. High research and building levels are the best way to store
  resources."

## Storage examples (verbatim — numbers, mechanics)

- Jump Gate Level 2 — "Long wait time, can store **4M Metal, 8M Crystal, 4M Deuterium**".
- High Level Nanite — "After level 4 can store **16M Metal, 8M crystal, 1.6M Deut**".
- Note: "resource buildings can be used to resource hide, but it is a riskier method especially if
  you have short build times from high Nanite Factories".

## Research ratios (verbatim — as stated)

- Ion Technology — "Research Ratios **10 : 3 : 1** (Great for Fleeters)" (metal : crystal : deuterium).
- Armour Technology — "for all Metal storing".
- Computer Technology — "for mass amounts of Crystal and Deuterium".
- Combustion Drive — "for mass amounts of Metal and Deuterium".
- Laser Technology — "can be good for mass amounts of Metal and Crystal".
- Weapons Technology — "good for more Metal and less Crystal".
- Shielding Technology — "good for less Metal and more Crystal".

## Limitations

- **CONTESTED as mechanics:** the ~4-hour minimum, the Jump Gate / Nanite storage figures (4M/8M/4M;
  16M/8M/1.6M) and the research cost ratios. All depend on build/research cost and time tables a wiki
  cannot establish; confirm against the official Gameforge object data or the host code.
- **ANECDOTAL:** the "specifically good researches" list is player preference ("Great for Fleeters"),
  not a rule.
- **Outdated-likely:** the storage numbers are stated without a version; treat as unverified against
  the host's current cost tables.
