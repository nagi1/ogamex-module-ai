# What this source is good for

The taxonomy of **saving** (resources and fleets) and the stated rule that only building and research
can be saved by cancelling, while shipyard builds cannot.

## Page premise (verbatim)

> Saving is not an official function in the game, it is a strategy used by most players.

## Save while being attacked

**Save by Mission** — "Load your resources into transports and move the fleet elsewhere. The timing
and the speed of the fleet are of the essence here to save deuterium." If a debris field is available
and you have recyclers, you can move there and "recall the mission to get your ships and resources
back quickly."

- Pro stated: "One regains one's resources shortly after the battle, so the losses are limited to
  solar satellites and defense." / "The recyclers can mine the debris field in the time spent there."
- Con stated: "Especially in the early game, the transport capacity might not suffice for all of
  one's resources." / "Fleeing in a DF bears the risk of one being detected by a sensor phalanx."
  Stated mitigation: "deploy your fleet to your moon, if you have one."

**Save by building/researching** — "one can start upgrading a building or research a technology.
After the attack, the process can be aborted to gain the resources back."

- **DOCUMENTED** — "Building ships or defenses in the shipyard is not recommended, because the build
  orders in the shipyard can't be aborted." But "one can save infinite resources by building ships"
  as a last resort: "it is better to get a few ships than to be raided and let the enemy have the
  resources."
- Pro: "Large, virtually infinite amounts of resources can be saved."
- Con: "Not always possible (building already in progress, or even worse, the research lab is being
  upgraded, i.e.: no research and no building possible)."

## Save while being absent

- **DOCUMENTED** — main method: fleetsaving (points to the Fleetsave article).
- **DOCUMENTED** — "vacation mode - the safest method": "only for long term use, since the vacation
  mode needs to be active for at least **3 days** and mines don't produce in that time."
