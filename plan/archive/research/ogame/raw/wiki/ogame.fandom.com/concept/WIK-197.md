## Stated technique (verbatim)
- Disbanding an alliance means to delete the alliance and all still within the alliance lose the tag.
- In other words, if a leader goes to Administrate alliance and clicks the Disband button, he will get a warning asking if he really wants to and if he says yes, the alliance is gone.
