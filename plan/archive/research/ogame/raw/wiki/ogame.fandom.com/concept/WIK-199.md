## Stated technique (verbatim)
- An NAP or Non Aggression Pact is an agreement between alliances or between an alliance and a player that states that neither will attack the other. These are enforced only by the alliances and/or players concerned, and are often broken for many reasons.
- There has been much debate over the abbreviation 'NAP' as it differs only in one letter to the internet slang term "NAB", similar to noob or newbie.
- It is a widely held view in the ogame community that only NABS do Naps as a NAB would have difficulty protecting him or herself.
- Many alliances have rules preventing its members from attacking and/or spying
- on each other. In a way, these can be considered as NAPs, but typically aren't
- called NAPs. Many people consider that no fighting within an alliance is a good
- idea and it is discouraged to attack a fellow alliance member.
