# What this source is good for

The **cancel-refund rule** that reactive saving depends on: which production can be cancelled and
what is refunded.

## Rule as stated (verbatim)

> It is possible to cancel the production of buildings or the research of technologies. When a
> production or a research is canceled, the resources that had been subtracted from the planet are
> given back.

> Note: ''Shipyard and defense builds can not be canceled.''

## Claims

- **DOCUMENTED (use)** — "This can be used as a good method for saving resources. It is possible to
  save resources from plunder by first starting a research or production and then cancel them when
  the danger passes."
- **CONTESTED (mechanics)** — that a cancelled building/research refunds the resources, and that
  shipyard/defence builds cannot be cancelled. A wiki cannot supply a mechanics claim; confirming
  needs the official Gameforge rules or the host implementation (the host's build/research queue
  behaviour).

## Limitations

- The page is a **stub** ("{{stub}}") and gives no numbers, costs or time limits.
- Refund completeness (full vs partial) is not stated beyond "are given back".
- Agrees with `Save` (WIK-022) and `Resource Saving` (WIK-027); the agreement is supporting
  evidence, not a mechanics confirmation.
