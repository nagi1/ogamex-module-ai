# What this source is good for

The definition of the **miner** play style and the rule that a pure miner always takes the
highest-efficiency (shortest-return-on-investment) upgrade. It also states the miner / turtle /
fleeter boundary, which is the classification this corpus needs.

## Position stated (verbatim)

> A **Miner** is a type of player that spends most of his or her resources on actions that will
> increase income. The miner often believes that any resource not spent on something that will pay
> itself back is a wasted resource. This is a more relaxed play style than others as it does not
> require constant time and attention.

> A pure miner will always do the highest efficiency upgrade.

## Claims

- **DOCUMENTED** — miner focuses on: building mines, often to a very high level over time;
  researching more levels of Astrophysics to make more colonies; researching more levels of Plasma
  Technology to increase mine production.
- **DOCUMENTED** — pure miners avoid spending on: too much defense (they fleetsave and promptly
  spend their resources instead); technologies that do not increase income (such as those needed for
  warships); high levels of automation (Robotics or Nanite) because they must save for upgrades
  later anyway and do not need faster building.
- **DOCUMENTED** — boundary rule: "If a miner starts to build lots of defenses to keep their
  resources safe, the play style becomes more of a Turtle." Stated reason: defenses cannot increase
  income, so a turtle is less focused on ROI than a miner.
- **DOCUMENTED** — transition rule: at a high mine level the next upgrade "might take many months
  or even years to see a return on investment"; at that point miners often switch to Turtle or
  Fleeter. Some continue building; very old universes allow "truly high levels of mines".
- **DOCUMENTED** — high mine levels need many fields, so a miner may be forced to terraform a
  planet that does not have enough.
- **ANECDOTAL** — "Some top miners only need to check on their accounts once or twice per day, or
  less."

## Limitations

- **Lifeform-era (v7+) content — recorded as a limitation, not a rule:** "since version 7 many
  miners also build a fleet for expeditions. As a result, a miner may often choose Discoverer class
  instead of Collector". This belongs to the class/Lifeform ruleset; do not assume class selection
  exists on the target ruleset without confirming the host version.
- The mechanics it names (Plasma Technology raising production, Robotics/Nanite as automation) are
  community phrasing. A **mechanics** claim from this page would be **CONTESTED** and would need the
  official Gameforge building/technology descriptions or the host code to confirm.
- No numbers are stated on this page; there is no ratio here to reuse.
