# What this source is good for

The income taxonomy an account plans against: it enumerates the ways resources enter an account.

## The six ways (verbatim list)

> There are 6 ways of gaining resources:

1. **Basic Income.** "Every planet has a basic income of a small amount of metal and crystal but
   not deuterium."
2. **Mining.** "Building and adding levels to Metal Mines, Crystal Mines, and Deuterium
   Synthesizers increase the hourly production of resources."
3. **Harvesting** debris fields with a recycler. "Available debris fields can be seen from the
   Galaxy View."
4. **Attacking** planets or moons.
5. **Trading** with other people in the game for a profit.
6. **Expeditions.** "This is done by sending a fleet to planet position 16. Expeditions can reward
   you with resources, ships or dark matter, or you can lose part of your fleet or all, or nothing
   happens."

## Claims

- **DOCUMENTED** — the enumeration itself, and the stated ordering (basic income and mining first,
  then harvesting, attacking, trading, expeditions).
- **DOCUMENTED** — "not deuterium" for basic income: i.e. the free/basic income is metal + crystal
  only.
- **DOCUMENTED (structural)** — expeditions are reached by sending a fleet to **planet position 16**.

## Limitations

- **CONTESTED as mechanics:** the exact basic-income amount ("a small amount"), the expedition
  outcome table, and the position-16 mechanism are community statements. Confirming them needs the
  official Gameforge rules/description or the host code — a wiki cannot supply a mechanics claim.
- The page is a stub-level taxonomy: **no numeric thresholds, ratios or costs** are given (the only
  number is the expedition position, 16).
- The Expedition link points off-site to `ogame.wikia.com`, not to a page evidenced here.
