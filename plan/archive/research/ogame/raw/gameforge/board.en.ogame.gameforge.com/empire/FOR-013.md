# Recommended colony setup?

A new miner asks where to put colonies and whether to spread them out or keep them together. The
thread is worth having because the community does not agree: the same question gets "spread out"
from one player and "keep them in the same system" from another, and both explain which playstyle
each answer serves. That disagreement is the doctrine — a placement rule has to pick a side.

## Stated technique (verbatim)

- I've read the pros and cons of spreading out my colonies vs keeping them together but I'm still not sure what would be better suited to a playstyle like mine. I did notice that a lot of players keep their colonies within a few systems of each other so I'm wondering if that's best for a uni like this?
- Personally I'm a fan of having my colonies well spread out.
- Right now you're likely making most of your progress from farming inactives - it therefore makes sense to find systems rich on those to place your additional colonies in.
- spread it out. i do 3 planets in a galaxy so to have farming faster. also if you are a miner and someone finds your planet profitable you will be attacked constantly. fleeters will just move a mobile planet near your cluster planets and boom you will become a farm
- Stacked planets (1 or a few more systems) are good if you want to play but you really don't want to invest much time. You could login once a day, transfer resources in a very short time, start some construction etc. As long as you're active and don't have a pile of resources no one will bother with you. If you don't have time for transport then just login and spend 2 minutes to spend all res on defence. If you choose to go this way just don't colonize slots 1-3 because they are the worst for production.
- The best setup is spreaded out in galaxies and apart in systems if they are in the same galaxy (at least 150 systems apart).
- Most importantly, colonize only slot 8 because it gives the biggest planets and highest metal production bonus. Don't keep anything lower than 200 which is a bare minimum for long term play. 250+ is highly recommended.
- Explore the systems and find available slot 8's with good inactives in the system (and no very strong players). This way you can farm those in-system inactives with very little time investment.
- 3 per galaxy spreaded, but Im not an expert
- I've mined for a long time and my best advice to you is if you want to make sure you have fast transports, easy defense and keep deut costs down, keep the planets in the same system and colonize slot 8 first with 9 and 10 soon after and finally 7, 6. Those provide you with the metal bonuses and 8 is the most.
- If you want, you can stick more planets in the same system but unless you plan on using DM for a relocation, you can only really colonize slots 4-11 because you will for sure want planets to have at least 200 fields. AT LEAST! Slots 4-11 all have a chance although, slot 4 and 11 are a little tougher to find at 200+ depending on uni settings.
- Another option if you just want the most production but want to keep relatively close is to spread planets to the system immediately right or left of your current system. For this you pretty much only look at slot 8 or if you want something that has a little more deut production (not really worth it but your call) you could use 9 or 10. You will rinse and repeat this for every colo so that your flight times are kept as low as possible while not being in the same system. For example, if you have a planet at 1:100:8 your colo will be at 1:101:8 or 1:99:8.
- One more piece of advice: Establish your colo and build it up. Get Astro for another colo or few colos fast and DO NOT build up your current Homeworld. The starter planet is always small and trust me, you will run out of fields.
