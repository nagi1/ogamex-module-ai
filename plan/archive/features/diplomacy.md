## What this file must not contain

- Any numeric threshold, distance, duration, count or ratio.

## Why it carries no doctrine

The module holds no opinion on diplomacy, and the source states no numbers, so there is nothing
to encode as constants, stances or weights. Anything numeric written here would be an invention
rather than a fact taken from the source.
