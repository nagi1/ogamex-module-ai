  blocks only the *guide* reading, not the host facts.

  (and by extension the other `board.origin` `Thread/` anchors cited 14 September) now **redirect to the
  forum root** (`forum.origin.ogame.gameforge.com`). These anchors must be re-found via forum search or
  the Wayback Machine before their domains (fleet composition, moon) can be deep-read.
- **Consequence for Stage 1**: discovery for fleet composition, moon, phalanx and ACS is genuinely
  required — the recorded anchors are stale. Re-source via `forum.origin.ogame.gameforge.com` search,
  `web.archive.org`, and the already-loading Fandom `Ships`/`Rapid_Fire` pages.
- **Recovered in the research pass (15 Sep):** `Tactic 05a — Fleet composition` and `Tutorial 15 — Moon`
  were recovered via the Wayback Machine (captures 2024-04-20 / 2024-05-19) and deep-read; Fandom
  `Rapid_Fire` loads via `?action=raw` (the canonical URL still redirects to the ad server); Gameforge,
  Sidian and ogames.net guides all loaded and were mined. Remaining discovery gap: **ACS** (ORG-009/010
  still redirect — not yet re-sourced).
